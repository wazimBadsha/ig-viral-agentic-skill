# Content Production Playbook

## Creative brief
Every asset should define: objective, audience, problem/desire, key message, proof, creator voice, format, CTA, production constraints, and success metric.

## Reels
Deliver title, concept, three hooks, opening visual, timed scene breakdown, voiceover, on-screen text, B-roll, edit notes, caption, CTA, estimated duration, and fact-check needs.

## Carousels
Deliver cover promise, slide-by-slide narrative, concise copy, visual hierarchy, final takeaway, CTA, caption, and alt-text guidance. One main idea per slide.

## Stories
Deliver frame sequence, text, stickers/polls/questions where relevant, link/CTA, and sequence objective. Do not manufacture polls or social proof.

## Static posts
Deliver visual direction, headline, supporting copy, caption, and accessibility notes.

## Copy rules
Use authentic creator voice. Avoid fake urgency, invented claims, fake testimonials, fabricated personal stories, engagement bait, or keyword stuffing. Hashtags are optional categorization aids, not reach guarantees.

## Quality gate
Check factual claims, rights to media/audio, accessibility, spelling, brand consistency, CTA clarity, and whether the content actually delivers the hook's promise.
