# Instagram Niche Research

Build a research brief for the requested niche, geography, and language.

## Research
- Define audience segments by needs, skill level, intent, and context; do not infer sensitive traits.
- Identify relevant creators and brands using available public search/browser sources.
- Review recent posts, recurring themes, audience questions, comment discussions, formats, and positioning.
- Capture source URLs, dates, visible metrics, and search date.
- Distinguish public counters from creator-provided analytics.
- Include smaller credible accounts as well as large accounts to reduce survivorship bias.

## Analysis
Map audience jobs-to-be-done, pain points, misconceptions, aspirations, vocabulary, objections, content saturation, and underserved subtopics. Compare competitors on positioning, formats, proof, audience, and differentiation.

## Deliverables
Research scope and sample; audience segments; competitor matrix; recurring topics; content gap map; 10 opportunity hypotheses; suggested content pillars; sources and limitations. Never fabricate market size, engagement, demographics, or private analytics.
