# Instagram Hook Lab

Generate hooks tailored to audience, topic, format, creator voice, and objective.

## Hook families
- Specific problem / desired outcome
- Myth versus reality
- Mistake and consequence
- Contrarian but defensible point
- Curiosity gap with honest payoff
- Before-and-after process
- Unexpected demonstration
- Short story / personal lesson (only if true)
- Checklist / diagnostic
- Question the audience already asks

## Quality checks
Every hook must be clear, truthful, specific, audience-relevant, and deliverable by the content. Avoid fabricated statistics, false urgency, clickbait, shame, and unsupported guarantees.

## Output
Provide 10–20 varied hooks grouped by angle. For the strongest 3, explain the target viewer, curiosity/tension mechanism, expected payoff, opening visual, and test variable. Keep wording original; do not imitate a named creator's signature phrasing.
