# Instagram Content Calendar

Create a realistic schedule based on the creator's capacity, goals, audience, and available assets.

## Planning logic
- Define the primary objective and supporting metrics.
- Choose 3–5 content pillars based on audience needs and positioning.
- Balance discovery, education, trust, community, and conversion.
- Mix formats according to production capacity.
- Include recurring series and repurposing opportunities.
- Avoid asserting universal best posting times; use account-specific Insights when available.

## Calendar fields
Date/time/timezone; pillar; topic; format; target audience; hook; creative brief; caption/CTA; asset owner; production status; approval status; publish status; metric to observe.

## Output
7-day or 30-day calendar; rationale; batching plan; asset checklist; fallback posts; measurement plan. Treat calendar entries as planned, not published.
